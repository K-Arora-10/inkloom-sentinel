# PRODUCT SPECIFICATION — INKLOOM SENTINEL
**CodeNexus India 2026 | Team ShortKut**

## 1. Product Overview
- **Name:** Inkloom Sentinel
- **Tagline:** Version. Test. Approve. Protect.
- **Organization Target:** NexaPay Technologies (Synthetic digital payments demonstration)
- **Team Members:** Krish Arora & Sanchita Sharma

## 2. Core Problem & Value Proposition
Enterprises publish marketing collateral, technical documentation, social media posts, and customer notices across decentralized teams, external creative agencies, and generative AI tools. This creates brand erosion, financial non-compliance, contradictory claims, and tone inconsistencies.

Inkloom Sentinel is not a generative content creator or generic grammar checker. It is a **deterministic and AI-assisted brand governance platform** that enforces brand fidelity, fact accuracy, and legal compliance before publication, sealing authorized releases with Ed25519 cryptographic signatures.

## 3. End-to-End Governance Lifecycle
```
[Content Submitted] -> [Deterministic Audit & Fact Matching] -> [AI Contextual Analysis]
         |
         v
[Explainable Findings & Vault Citations] -> [Minimal Fix Assistant] -> [Version Bump]
         |
         v
[Reviewer Sign-Off & Ed25519 Sealing] -> [Gateway Verification & Tamper Prevention]
```

## 4. Key Personas & Roles
1. **Creator (Krish Arora):** Drafts submissions, uploads documents (PDF/DOCX/Text), views audit findings, applies minimal fixes, and submits revised content for sign-off.
2. **Reviewer (Sanchita Sharma):** Authorized compliance reviewer who inspects findings, requests modifications, approves content, and generates cryptographically signed release certificates.
