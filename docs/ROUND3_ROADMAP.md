# ROUND 3 ARCHITECTURAL ROADMAP — INKLOOM SENTINEL

The Round 2 product delivers a complete, verified textual brand governance pipeline with cryptographic Ed25519 seals. The following three major architectural advancements are scheduled for Round 3:

## Improvement 1: Multimodal Brand Integrity
- **Automated Logo Verification:** Validates logo clear space, aspect ratio preservation, and minimum pixel dimensions.
- **Brand Color Palette Analysis:** Extracts dominant colors from uploaded images and banners, calculating perceptual color difference (Delta-E) against official #405746 and #F8F7F4 tokens.
- **WCAG 2.1 Accessibility:** Validates color contrast ratios for foreground text over brand background surfaces.
- **Visual Typography Auditing:** Uses OCR bounding boxes to ensure banner typography adheres to the approved brand hierarchy.

## Improvement 2: Intelligent Brand Truth Management
- **Advanced Contradiction Detection:** Employs multi-hop semantic reasoning to detect subtle discrepancies across dispersed regulatory filings and marketing claims.
- **Claim Passport Expiry Lifecycle:** Automated notification triggers and review queue deprecation when source audit evidence expires.
- **Source Conflict Resolution:** Hierarchical precedence rules between corporate board charters, CFO pricing memos, and regional marketing copy.
- **Blast-Radius Impact Simulation:** Simulates the cascading impact across all historical and pending submissions whenever an approved fact (e.g. ₹399 fee) changes.

## Improvement 3: Governance Intelligence & Analytics
- **Recurring Violation Analytics:** Cluster detection identifying specific departments, agencies, or AI prompts that repeatedly fail brand rules.
- **Ambiguous Guideline Identification:** Identifies brand rules with high reviewer override rates, signaling that the underlying policy is poorly drafted.
- **Agency Turnaround Analytics:** SLA metrics between Creator draft submission, Fix Assistant acceptance, and Reviewer cryptographic sign-off.
- **Exportable Board Audits:** Generates exportable compliance reports with verifiable Ed25519 hash trees.
